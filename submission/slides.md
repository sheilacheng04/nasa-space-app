## 1. A place and a question
Show the real, dated Angat regional HH quicklook. Ask whether the shoreline is changing. Caption PROVISIONAL, CRID P05023, downsampled browse, not calibrated. No shrinkage number.

## 2. What NISAR sees
Explain HH, HV and the conditional HH/HV ratio. Smooth water can be dark; vegetation, roughness and geometry complicate interpretation. State that this is typical SAR behavior, not a site finding.

## 3. The available record
Show the real before/after swipe and acquisition table. Print n, date, maturity and CRID. Mark the July–August gap. The calibrated water-area and backscatter charts have n = 0. Explain the unavailable authenticated processing.

## 4. Two climate hypotheses
Place Luzon/Angat alongside Lower Piura. Compare monthly ERA5 weather with 2025 and 1991–2020. Add NOAA context. Human operations and seasonal timing are competing explanations. Dadu is a separate method test; 2022 was a La Niña year.

## 5. Evidence the audience can inspect
