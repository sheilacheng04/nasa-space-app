# Heuristic UX Review (`docs/UX_REVIEW.md`)

This document records the heuristic evaluation of all five pages of **The Two-Faced Pacific** against six core usability heuristics:
1. **Visibility of System Status**
2. **Plain Language & Match with Real World**
3. **Consistency & Standards**
4. **Error Prevention & Scientific Guardrails**
5. **Recognition Rather Than Recall**
6. **Help, Progressive Disclosure & Wayfinding**

---

## Summary of Findings & Resolutions

| ID | Page | Heuristic | Finding | Severity | Status | Resolution Implemented |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **UX-01** | Story (`#story`) | Visibility of System Status | `.pattern-effects` in `src/story.css` initialized at `opacity: 0` and `src/story.js` waited for `IntersectionObserver` before drawing, causing a visually blank canvas on slow connections or before scroll. | **High** | **Fixed** | Set `.pattern-effects` initial opacity to `.72`, initialized `inView = true` in `src/story.js`, and switched `#story-sites` thumbnails to eager decoding (`loading="eager"`). |
| **UX-02** | Globe (`#globe`) | Visibility of System Status | When WebGL was disabled or unavailable, `src/canvas-globe.js` did not invoke `draw()` synchronously on initialization or resize, leaving the 2D compatibility canvas temporarily blank. | **High** | **Fixed** | Added synchronous `draw()` calls on init, resize, resume, and surface image load in `src/canvas-globe.js`, plus a rich loading skeleton in `GlobePage.tsx`. |
| **UX-03** | All Pages | Help & Wayfinding | First-time visitors landed on a long narrative without a quick 3-step overview of how `Story`, `Sites`, `Globe`, `Media`, and `Data and method` fit together, and pages lacked a consistent closing `"Where to next"` signpost. | **High** | **Fixed** | Added a dismissable 3-step `FirstVisitGuide` banner persisted in `localStorage` (`pacific-first-visit-guide-dismissed-v1`), a `?` button (`#open-guide`) in the header to reopen it anytime, a 1-sentence page purpose at the top of every page, and a `WhereToNext` block at the bottom of every page. |
| **UX-04** | Story, Sites, Globe | Visibility of System Status | Dated NISAR radar frames required manual clicking to advance, lacked a `"Frame X of Y"` counter and progress bar on Story/Sites, and did not show a running summary of total observations (`"57 real observations across 4 places"`). | **High** | **Fixed** | Added automatic progression when >=50% visible (disabled when `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`), hover/focus/touch/keyboard pause, speed selector, loop toggle, `"Frame X of Y"` badge, progress bar, maturity badge, explicit Jul 27–Aug 10 gap notice, frame prefetching, and a Guided Tour. |
| **UX-05** | All Pages | Plain Language | Technical acronyms (`SAR`, `NISAR`, `backscatter`, `HH/HV`, `GCOV`, `GIBS`, `provisional`, `CRID`, `ERA5`, `reanalysis`, `ONI`, `Niño 3.4`, `MODIS`, `NDVI-style greenness`) appeared in prose without inline definitions, and `MethodPage` lead sentences averaged Flesch reading ease < 50. | **High** | **Fixed** | Created `GlossaryTerm.tsx` providing keyboard- and touch-accessible inline popovers linking to `#method/glossary`, rewrote lead copy across all 5 pages to Flesch reading ease >= 60 and average sentence length < 20 words, and added `tests/readability.test.js`. |
| **UX-06** | Story, Sites, Globe, Media | Recognition Rather Than Recall | Figures and charts lacked a uniform caption format explaining what is shown, where, the date/source, how to read the visual scale, and what it cannot prove. | **Medium** | **Fixed** | Created `FigureCaption.tsx` (`"What am I looking at?"` 2-line default summary + expandable `"How to read it & what it can't tell you"`) and applied it across Story, Sites, Media, and Method figures. |
| **UX-07** | Sites (`#sites`) | Recognition & Wayfinding | Sites lacked a breadcrumb trail (`Sites › Place › Date`), only listed 4 places instead of global El Niño/La Niña comparison places, and lacked direct cross-links to view the active place on the Globe or Media pages. | **High** | **Fixed** | Added `Sites › <Place> › <Date>` breadcrumb, bidirectional cross-page links (`#globe?site=...&date=...`, `#media/<place>`, `#method/evidence`, `#story`), a 34-place global selector (`#global-place-select`), and explicit `"Radar frames not saved for this place"` + ASF Vertex polygon search link for the 30 places without saved WebP frames. |
| **UX-08** | Globe (`#globe`) | Visibility of System Status & Control | The Globe lacked a unified status strip showing the active Mode, Layer, Date, and Place, prevented deep zoom below altitude `0.08`, and lacked a 1-click Philippines preset and local GIBS map handoff. | **High** | **Fixed** | Added `globe-status-strip` with Mode, Layer, Date, Place, `"Reset view"`, `"Philippines focus"`, `"Guided tour"`, and `"Local GIBS map"` handoff (with `"← Back to globe"`), and lowered minimum altitude to `0.02` (`minDistance = 102`). |
| **UX-09** | Story, Sites, Globe, Media | Consistency & Local Relevance | Headlines were only viewable as a global list on `#media`; picking a place on Story, Sites, or Globe did not show local news matched to that place. | **High** | **Fixed** | Generated `public/data/news-by-place.json` (494 place-matched links from the 293 saved headlines) and built `PlaceNewsPanel.tsx` showing `"News about <Place>"` with wording-tone caveats, or `"No saved headlines for this place yet"` + disabled `"Search live"` button (ready for Prompt 2). |
| **UX-10** | Media (`#media`) | Information Architecture | The Media page opened directly into filter controls without a high-level visual summary of how coverage and wording tone distribute across regions. | **Medium** | **Fixed** | Added a **Media & Regional Overview Dashboard** (`#media-dashboard`) with KPI cards, a visual tone distribution bar, and interactive regional breakdown cards above the headline archive. |
| **UX-11** | Data & Method (`#method`) | Error Prevention & Simplicity | The optional CSV upload section (`#my-data` / `DataPage.tsx`) cluttered the Method page and confused visitors about whether uploaded points were official satellite records. | **High** | **Fixed** | Removed the CSV upload feature (`#my-data`) from `App.tsx` and `MethodPage.tsx` and added 5 visual data source summary cards above the expandable technical ledgers. |
