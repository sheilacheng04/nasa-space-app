# Feature & Control Audit (`docs/FEATURES.md`)

This audit catalogs every interactive control, panel, and feature across all five pages of **The Two-Faced Pacific**, recording its data source, initial status during the pre-improvement audit, severity, and the fix implemented in Prompt 1.

| Page | Control / Feature | What It Does | Data Source | Initial Status | Severity | Fix Implemented |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Global Header** | Skip link (`.skip-link`) | Moves keyboard focus directly to `#main` without changing the URL hash | None (DOM focus) | Clear | Low | Preserved keyboard focus behavior across all routes. |
| **Global Header** | Primary navigation (`1 Story` – `5 Data and method`) | Switches top-level views and updates `aria-current="page"` | URL hash (`#story`, `#sites`, `#globe`, `#media`, `#method`) | Clear | Medium | Preserved 5-page order, visible active indicator, and `aria-current="page"`. |
| **Global Header** | First-visit 3-step guide button (`#open-guide`, `?`) | Reopens the dismissable 3-step orientation banner at any time | `localStorage` (`pacific-first-visit-guide-dismissed-v1`) | Missing | Medium | Added `?` button in header and dismissable 3-step `FirstVisitGuide` banner on first visit. |
| **Global Header** | Command search (`#open-search`, `Ctrl/Cmd+K`) | Opens modal search across study sites, 34 global places, 909 Natural Earth cities, and 293 headlines | `sites.json`, `places.json`, `gazetteer.json`, `news.json` | Unclear (only searched 4 sites + gazetteer) | Medium | Extended search to include all 34 global study places (`places.json`) alongside the 909 bundled Natural Earth places. |
| **Global Header** | Theme toggle (`#toggle-theme`) | Switches between Light and Dark themes while keeping `--blue` and `--orange` AA contrast | `localStorage` (`pacific-theme`) | Clear | Low | Preserved theme persistence and verified >= 4.5:1 contrast in both themes. |
| **1 · Story** | Sticky Chapter Progress Bar (`.story-chapter-bar`) | Shows the 5 Story chapters, highlights the active chapter on scroll, and displays `"Chapter X of 5"` | IntersectionObserver on `#ocean`, `#land`, `#reading-radar`, `#story-sites`, `#local` | Missing | High | Added sticky chapter bar with live scroll progress indicator. |
| **1 · Story** | Hero NISAR Observation Card (`.hero-observation`) | Displays the latest saved Angat NISAR GIBS preview with maturity badge and standard caption | `public/data/evidence.json` + saved WebP asset | Unclear (caption lacked structured "How to read it") | Medium | Added standard 2-line `FigureCaption` with expandable `"How to read it & what it can't tell you"`. |
| **1 · Story** | Ocean Basin Interactive Stage (`#ocean-canvas`, `.ocean-stage-controls`) | Animates normal trade winds vs. El Niño warm-water shift; toggles wind, rain, labels, and pause | Conceptual illustration (`src/story.js`, `src/story.css`) | Broken under slow IO (`.pattern-effects` started at `opacity: 0`, waited for IO) | High | Set `.pattern-effects` initial opacity to `.72` and initialized `inView = true` in `src/story.js` so canvas draws immediately. |

| **1 · Story** | Regional NISAR Preview Autoplay & Guided Tour (`#story-preview-player`) | Autoplays dated radar previews across the 4 radar sites when >=50% visible, shows `"Frame X of Y"`, progress bar, maturity, and Jul 27–Aug 10 gap, plus a Guided Tour | `public/data/sites/{id}/evidence.json` | Missing | High | Built `StoryPreviewPlayer.tsx` with >=50% visibility autoplay, hover/focus/touch pause, speed selector, explicit gap display, and Guided Tour. |
| **1 · Story** | Philippines Focus Section (`#philippines-focus`) | Highlights 6 Philippine study places (`angat`, `luzon`, `cagayan-valley`, `mindoro`, `cotabato-mindanao`, `iloilo-panay`) with direct links to Sites, Globe, and local news | `public/data/places.json`, `public/data/news-by-place.json` | Missing | High | Added dedicated Philippines Focus section on Story and preset on Globe while keeping global view as default. |
| **1 · Story** | Overall Impact by Region Summary (`#overall-impact-summary`) | Summarizes real counts (study places, saved radar frames, matched headlines) across 6 global regions | `public/data/places.json`, `public/data/news-by-place.json` | Missing | High | Added regional summary table and cards using real counts rather than modeled colors. |
| **1 · Story** | Story Site Thumbnails (`#story-sites .evidence-row img`) | Links each of the 4 core radar sites to its dated timeline in `#sites/<id>` | `public/data/evidence.json` | Broken below fold (`loading="lazy"` left `naturalWidth === 0` before scroll) | High | Switched to eager decoding (`loading="eager"`) so all 4 thumbnails decode immediately on initial load. |
| **2 · Sites** | Breadcrumb & Cross-Page Bar (`.sites-breadcrumb`, `.cross-page-links`) | Shows `Sites › <Place> › <Date>` and links to `"See this place on the globe"`, `"See local news"`, `"See its sources"`, and `"Back to the story"` | URL hash (`#sites/<id>?date=...` / `#/sites?place=...`) | Missing | High | Added live breadcrumb and bidirectional cross-page links preserving place and date in the URL. |
