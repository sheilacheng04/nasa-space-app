2The ocean signal, in context

Scientists track sea-surface temperatures in the central Pacific. The weekly Niño 3.4 index and the three-month ONI describe different time periods. An ocean index sets the context; it cannot tell us whether a particular field is dry.

Chapter 6

5Where NISAR radar comes in

Through clouds, day or night

Radar sends its own microwave pulses. Moisture, vegetation and surface roughness shape the returning signal.

Watch water and land

Compare polarizations, dates and valid pixels. Wind, crops and flooded vegetation can complicate the appearance of water.

Keep the evidence separate

Temperature comes from climate data. Radar backscatter is a different measurement with its own limits.

A local story / Philippines

Water connects a rice field to a city.

The questions are close to home. The evidence needs to be just as grounded.

In Central Luzon, the timing of water matters as much as the amount. A flooded paddy can mark the start of a crop cycle. At Angat, a moving shoreline raises a different question: how are rainfall and reservoir operations changing the water footprint?

These are the places our Philippine study follows. We put rainfall, radar and local knowledge side by side, and keep the explanation open until the evidence agrees.
